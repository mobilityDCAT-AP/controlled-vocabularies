Transport Mode: Description back to ToC

Controlled vocabulary for the transport modes associated with a mobility dataset. The taxonomy is organised in two hierarchy levels. The first level represents the type of transport mode (e.g., rail transport, road transport, air transport, water transport, special transport, micromobility). The second level concretises the first level via one or several transport modes. It is recommended to select second level values for better specificity. The following nested list is implemented:
  • Road transport
    • Car
    • Motorcycle
    • Bus
    • Coach
    • Truck
  • Rail transport
    • Long-distance rail
    • Regional and local rail
    • Tram and light rail
    • Metro
  • Water transport
    • Maritime
    • Inland waterways
    • Shuttle ferry
    • Water taxi
  • Air transport
    • Aeroplane
    • Air taxi
  • Special transport
    • Cableways
    • Elevator
    • Escalator
    • Seasonal transport
  • Micromobility
    • Bicycle and E-Bike
    • E-Scooter
    • Moped
  • Pedestrian
  • Other
